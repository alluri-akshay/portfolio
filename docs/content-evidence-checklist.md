## Claims requiring clarification

The earlier 60% and 1,000+ quantitative claims have been removed from the public
portfolio. Provide evidence below if they should be included in a future update.

- [ ] **Auto Auth: approximately 60% less manual data-entry effort.** Provide
  the baseline workflow, measured workflow, unit (time/actions/fields), sample
  size, and who measured the result. If unavailable, use a qualitative account
  of prefill and validation instead of a percentage.
- [ ] **TaskLabs: 1,000+ active users.** Identify the platform/source and date;
  present this as the platform's audience with your own work described separately.
- [ ] **Education: 7.88 CGPA.** Confirm final/current value and grading scale.

## Editorial rules for later phases

- Use confirmed facts only; do not infer ownership from organization membership.
- Omit unavailable demo links rather than presenting inactive buttons.
- Describe contribution and engineering decisions even when numerical results
  are unavailable.
- Real project screenshots must show the actual product. Decorative generated
  images must never stand in for evidence of shipped work.
- Mark incomplete items here; do not publish this checklist as visitor-facing UI.
